Opening vignette

“More Than Food and Holidays”

Jordan: “When I hear culture, I usually think about food, music, holidays, clothing, or traditions. Isn’t that mostly what culture means?”

Aaliyah: “Those things can be part of culture, but culture goes deeper. It can shape how families communicate, comfort children, show respect, view independence, and relate to teachers.”

Maya: “So culture is not just something children bring for a classroom celebration. It affects how they learn, relate to others, and understand themselves.”

Jordan: “If teachers only notice the visible parts, they might miss the deeper parts.”

Aaliyah: “Exactly. Teachers have to learn from families, not just make assumptions about them.”

Maya: “Maybe the challenge is to see culture as part of everyday development, not as something extra we add to the classroom.”

Think first: How can educators move beyond surface-level celebrations and become responsive to the deeper cultural experiences of children and families?
