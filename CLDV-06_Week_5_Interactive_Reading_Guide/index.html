Opening vignette

“What Does the Classroom Say?”

Jordan: “This classroom looks welcoming. There are colorful posters, books, toys, and learning centers. Isn’t that what a good classroom environment should look like?”

Aaliyah: “It might be a good start. But I wonder whose languages, families, cultures, and everyday experiences are reflected here.”

Maya: “So we’re not just asking whether the room is attractive. We’re asking whether children can see themselves, their families, and their communities in the materials and activities.”

Jordan: “I guess a classroom could look cheerful but still not feel familiar or meaningful to every child.”

Aaliyah: “And being developmentally appropriate matters too. The materials need to match children’s ages, abilities, interests, languages, and ways of learning.”

Maya: “The classroom environment teaches children before the teacher even says anything.”

Think first: How can educators design environments, materials, and activities that are developmentally appropriate while also reflecting children’s cultures, languages, families, and communities?
